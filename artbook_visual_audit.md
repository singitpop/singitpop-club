## 🎨 Design Language & Consistency

### The "Male Lead" Rule
As confirmed on **2026-04-02**, the standard for SingIt Pop artbook visuals is a **Male Lead Singer** (typically in his 30s) as the primary focal point.
- **Exceptions:** *May In Motion* currently features a female lead on a balcony (as previously approved). 
- **Crowds/Backup:** Female characters are represented in crowds and background settings to maintain balance while keeping the "Popstar" identity consistent.

### Resolution & Format
- **Covers:** Square PNG, high-detail cinematic.
- **Desktop Wallpapers:** 16:9 Landscape, photorealistic.
- **Mobile Wallpapers:** 9:16 Portrait, cinematic lighting.

## ✍️ The Pitch (Draft)
*Paste this into the "Tell us about your song" section in Spotify for Artists.*

"Back Pocket Summer" is the anthem of that one perfect season you never want to let go. Built on a foundation of sun-drenched acoustic guitars and a 'slide and sway' rhythm, the track captures the feeling of a slow-falling sunset and the quiet memories we carry with us long after the heat fades. 

Recorded with an emphasis on organic, warm production, it bridges the gap between classic Nashville storytelling and a modern, high-end Geordie Country sound. It’s a song made for 'Golden Hour' playlists, long drives, and slow-dancing in a wheat field.

**Key Promotional Drivers:**
- **Dedicated 24/7 Radio**: The track is a lead single on the **'Country Signal'** radio broadcast (broadcasting live at singitpop.com/radio/live).
- **Social Strategy**: A coordinated TikTok and Instagram campaign using cinematic, 'Authentic Country' visuals to drive viral engagement.
- **Community**: Backed by the Singitpop global fan community with a heavy focus on the UK and US country markets.

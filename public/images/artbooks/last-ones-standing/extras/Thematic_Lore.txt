THEMATIC LORE: LAST ONES STANDING

Setting the Scene:
The sun drops low behind the dusty ridges, casting long, amber shadows across an immense, weathered wooden dance hall that sits miles from the nearest highway. Out here, time isn't measured by clocks, but by the rhythm of boot heels on hardwood and the steady thrum of an acoustic bass rolling through the humid night air. 

"Last Ones Standing" is a cinematic love letter to the endurance of neon-lit nights and the timeless tradition of line dancing under the stars. The album captures the feeling of a 'Back Pocket Summer'—that fleeting, golden season where the air is sweet, the music is loud, and tomorrow feels a million miles away. From the slow, syrupy cadence of 'July Lines' to the high-energy bursts of 'Boot Scoot Bounce', the record is built entirely around movement and connection.

Every track serves as a distinct set piece in this universe:
- The dim, glowing beer signs reflecting off the polished floor.
- The synchronized, hypnotic motion of strangers sliding in perfect time.
- The quiet, acoustic intimacy found in the back corners when the night finally winds down.

This collection represents the heart of modern country infused with a pulse-pounding, danceable energy. It is for those who stay until the lights come on—the true Last Ones Standing.

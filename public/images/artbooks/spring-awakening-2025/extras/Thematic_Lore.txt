Inspired by the natural symphony of the season, 'Spring Awakening' is an orchestral-pop masterpiece. This collection blends traditional symphonic arrangements with modern pop production to celebrate the return of life to the landscape. Each track is built around a sense of movement and metamorphosis, capturing the delicate beauty of a budding flower and the powerful surge of a rushing river. It's a lush, immersive experience that invites the listener to wake up and see the world anew.

When the sun dips below the skyline and the neon lights of the strip flicker to life, 'Night Drive' becomes the heartbeat of the city. Inspired by the cinematic synth-pop of the 1980s, this collection is a love letter to high-speed escapes and late-night reflections. From the pulsing rhythm of 'In the Groove Tonight' to the melancholic sweep of 'Sailing into the Sunset,' each track is designed to be heard through a car stereo with the windows down. It's the sound of a world that only wakes up after dark, where every turn in the road brings a new melody and every streetlight is a spotlight.

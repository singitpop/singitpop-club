THEMATIC LORE: THROUGH THE GLASS

Setting the Scene:
The view from inside a dark, quiet room looking out through a rain-streaked window. Outside, the blurred lights of an empty country gas station flicker against the dark sky. A coffee cup sits on the windowsill, its warmth providing a small anchor against the storm raging just inches away on the other side of the fragile pane.

"Through the Glass" is a deeply acoustic, introspective country record about observation, separation, and enduring love. It captures the quiet grace of small-town life—the front porch lights left on in the dark, the slow dance under a neon bar sign, and the unspoken promises kept over the years.

Every track tells a story of distance and connection:
- The quiet comfort of watching a storm from a safe place beside someone you love.
- The reflection of neon lights on wet pavement in an empty town.
- The invisible barriers we construct, and the warmth of finally breaking them down.

This collection is raw, stripped-back, and intensely personal. It’s the sound of a country songwriter looking at the world not from the center stage, but quietly, through the glass.

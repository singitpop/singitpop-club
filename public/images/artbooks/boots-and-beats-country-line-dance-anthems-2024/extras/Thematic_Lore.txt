Kick up the dust and find your place on the floor. 'Boots And Beats' is the ultimate high-energy collection of modern line dance anthems. Combining heavy-hitting rhythmic beats with traditional country fiddle and steel guitar, this album is designed for one thing: movement. From the infectious hook of 'Jeans Got the Boots' to the rapid-fire energy of 'Line Dance Fever,' every track is a call to the dancefloor. It's the sound of a Saturday night at the local honky-tonk, amplified for a new generation of dancers.

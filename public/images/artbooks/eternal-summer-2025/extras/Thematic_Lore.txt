Forget the calendar; in the world of 'Eternal Summer,' the sun never sets. This is a high-energy, feel-good pop collection designed to keep the summer vibes alive all year long. Featuring infectious choruses, tropical percussion, and a sense of unbridled joy, the album is a call to adventure and a celebration of youth. Whether you're actually at the beach or just dreaming of one, 'Eternal Summer' is the perfect companion for your next escape.

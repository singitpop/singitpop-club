THEMATIC LORE: LAST ONES STANDING

Setting the Scene:
A sprawling, authentic country honky-tonk dive bar sitting quiet at 2 AM. The massive, scuffed oak dance floor stretches out beneath the glow of dim, buzzing neon beer signs. It smells of aged whiskey, sawdust, and warm wood. The room speaks of stories lived loud and nights pushed past their limit.

"Last Ones Standing" is a modern country record that blends raw acoustic roots with the electrifying energy of a Saturday night dance hall. It captures the rhythm of line dancing, the pulse of a kick drum, and the sheer grit of outlasting the crowd.

Every track chronicles the life inside that bar:
- The smooth, steady count of a two-step across the floor.
- The electric tension between a couple refusing to let the night end.
- The raw, acoustic heart of the country beat when all the lights go down.

This collection represents the survival of the authentic country spirit—sharp, unpolished, and built for those who won't go home until the music stops.

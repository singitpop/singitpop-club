Designed for the most significant day of a person's life, 'Forever Starts Today' is a collection of timeless country ballads that celebrate love, commitment, and the promise of a shared future. Recorded with a mix of traditional acoustic instruments and modern cinematic production, these tracks provide the perfect backdrop for aisles, first dances, and departures. Each song is a testament to the enduring power of the heart, capturing the nervous excitement of 'Our Forever Starts Today' and the deep, settled peace of 'Right Where I Belong.' This is music for the moments that define us.

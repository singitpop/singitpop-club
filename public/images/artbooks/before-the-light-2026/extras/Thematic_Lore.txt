Before the light breaks over the Great Smoky Mountains, there is a moment of absolute stillness. 'Before The Light' is an exploration of that pre-dawn clarity—the hour of secrets, soft melodies, and the raw vulnerability of a songwriter at his piano. 

This album strips back the artifice of the music industry to reveal the pulse of the man beneath the spotlight, featuring acoustic arrangements that echo with the natural resonance of the Tennessee wilderness. It is a journey through the quiet hours, where every note is a breath and every lyric is a confession. Designed for the dreamers and the early risers, 'Before The Light' is the sound of hope emerging from the shadows.

THEMATIC LORE: BEFORE THE LIGHT

Setting the Scene:
The vast, arid expanse of a high desert sits under a massive, starry sky. Hours past midnight, the silence is broken by the distant, thumping bass of a massive underground Dance Pop rave hidden among the mesas. Neon lasers slash through columns of dust kicked up by thousands of synchronized dancers, turning the barren landscape into a futuristic oasis of sound and light.

"Before the Light" is an electronic odyssey built for the euphoric span of time between the peak of the night and the break of dawn. It channels the visceral energy of a 'Desert Pulse', driving relentless BPMs against vast, atmospheric synths. 

This record is a journey through different chambers of a massive, temporary city built purely for the night:
- High-intensity strobe rooms where the bass shakes the bedrock.
- Ambient chill-out tents glowing with warm LED hues against the cold desert sand.
- The surreal, collective anticipation as the crowd waits for the very first sliver of morning sun to hit the horizon.

This collection represents the pinnacle of communal energy—an unapologetic, neon-drenched celebration that demands you keep moving, right up until the moment before the light.

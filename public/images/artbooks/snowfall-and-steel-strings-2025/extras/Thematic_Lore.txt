Experience the magic of the season through a lens of starlight and song. 'Snowfall And Steel Strings' is a celebratory collection of holiday anthems that blend traditional warmth with modern pop energy. From the glitter of city lights on frozen pavement to the quiet, reflective moments by the fire, the album captures the multifaceted spirit of winter. It's a soundtrack for found family and the joy of coming home, wrapped in high-gloss production and heartfelt melody.

Welcome to the future as seen through the neon glow of 1985. 'Neon Dreams' is a maximalist explosion of synth-wave energy and futuristic pop ambition. This album takes the nostalgic sounds of the past and propels them into 2025 with cutting-edge production and high-concept storytelling. It's an odyssey through a digital landscape of light and sound, where every beat is a pulse and every melody is a neon sign. Get ready to dream in technicolor.

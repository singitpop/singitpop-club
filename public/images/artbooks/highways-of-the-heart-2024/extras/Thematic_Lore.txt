The road is a mirror for the soul. 'Highways Of The Heart' is a concept album about the journeys we take to find ourselves. Rooted in the traditions of American road-trip rock and folk-country, the music captures the feeling of the open road—the sense of possibility, the weight of the rearview mirror, and the rhythm of the tires on the asphalt. It's a collection for those who find peace in the transition, featuring sprawling arrangements and lyrics that look both forward and back.

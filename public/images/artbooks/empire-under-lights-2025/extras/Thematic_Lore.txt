In the city that never sleeps, the empire is built under the glow of a million lights. 'Empire Under Lights' is a high-concept pop-noir album that explores the ambition, the isolation, and the electric thrill of urban life. With dark, subterranean synth lines and sharp, rhythmic hooks, the music captures the pulse of the metropolis at midnight. It's a cinematic journey through the corridors of power and the hidden corners of the street, where everyone is chasing a dream and the lights never go out.

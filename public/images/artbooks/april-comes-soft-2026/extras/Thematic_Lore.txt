THEMATIC LORE: APRIL COMES SOFT

Setting the Scene:
The silent, fragile hours of early spring just before dawn. The location is an underground dance club exactly at 6 AM, stripped of its chaotic crowd. Slow-moving ambient laser lights slice through the lingering thin smoke. Outside, the rain has stopped, and the pale blue light of a new day shines through an open exit door, reflecting in puddles of water on the black pavement. 

"April Comes Soft" is an intensely emotional dance-pop record about the aftermath of a profound release. It captures the exact feeling of stepping out of a loud, energetic club and feeling the calm, cold morning air—the sensation of leaving heavy emotional baggage on the dance floor and watching the sun rise with a clear mind.

Every track represents a stage of that emotional arc:
- The initial, overwhelming pressure of the midnight crowd.
- The kinetic, hypnotic release of dancing without restraint.
- The serene, exhausted clarity of the morning after.

This collection represents shedding the winter. It’s an energetic purge wrapped in the softest, most peaceful resolution imaginable.

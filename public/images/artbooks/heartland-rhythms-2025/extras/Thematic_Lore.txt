Rooted in the soil of the American Midwest, 'Heartland Rhythms' is a celebratory return to the foundations of country-rock. This album is built on steady backbeats, rolling basslines, and the unmistakable sound of a Telecaster guitar. It's a collection of honest, hard-working anthems that honor the people and the places that keep the heart of the country beating. From small-town Friday nights to long drives through the cornfields, these are songs for the moments that feel like home.

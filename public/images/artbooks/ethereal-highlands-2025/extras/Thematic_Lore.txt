Drawing inspiration from the mist-covered peaks of the Scottish Highlands, 'Ethereal Highlands' is a hauntingly beautiful blend of Celtic folk and ambient pop. Featuring traditional instruments like the fiddle and whistle alongside ethereal synth pads, the album creates a sense of ancient mystery and timeless peace. It's an atmospheric escape into a world of legends and landscape, where the music carries the weight of history and the lightness of the mountain air.
